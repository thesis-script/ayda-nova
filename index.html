FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a prototype and an MVP?

A prototype shows how your product will look and feel — the screens and navigation are clickable, but the features are not connected to real data. An MVP is a working first version: real features, user accounts and a database, ready for your first users.

Do you build mobile apps as well as websites?

Yes. Both the Prototype and MVP plans are available for websites and for mobile apps, at the same prices.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the project. A prototype is usually the quickest; an MVP or a full product takes longer. You'll get a clear timeline with your quote before we start.

Can I upgrade my prototype to an MVP later?

Of course. Many projects start as a prototype to validate the idea, then grow into an MVP built on the same design.

What do I need to get started?

Just your idea. Send me an email describing what you want to build, and we'll figure out the rest together.
